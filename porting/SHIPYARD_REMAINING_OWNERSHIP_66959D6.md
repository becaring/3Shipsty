A local adapter is not automatically an unextracted platform implementation. The next migrations should move small mechanisms with explicit contracts while retaining layout, lifecycle, formula and observation policy in the consumers.

## Fast3D: ownership already established

Shipyard already owns the bounded VMX position transform, clip/matrix quantization, rounding probe and alias guard; exact normalized `UnitColor` and mechanical vertex-stream lowering; optional directional shade-cache mechanism; texture-binding certificate; triangle-state certificate/invalidation lowering; command stack, dispatch owner, opcode tables and triangle cull/emission splitting. Evidence includes P `sources/fast3d/{vertex_transform,clip_quantize}.{h,cpp}`, `unit_color.h`, `shade_cache.h`, `texture_bindings.h`, `triangle_pipeline.h`, and their `python/xbox360_platform` projections.

The two consumers' invocation/trait files correctly remain local where they encode layouts, source anchors, callback ordering, resource policy or distinct semantics. Shared ownership also does not imply both consumers adopt every optional optimization: modern does not currently use Smash's directional lighting pipeline or its shade-cache integration.

## Recommended next packages

1. **Small independent extractions:** immutable launch environment and exact Fast3D conversion/truncation/publication primitives. Carry their current tests and domains, with narrow consumer adapters. These have the clearest reusable boundaries.
2. **Culling/normal dedup and Prism lowering:** separate value-only cache kernels from observation/layout/rounding policy; build a version-aware Prism projector tested against both pinned inputs. Keep both builders' resource semantics local.
3. **Diagnostic worker:** establish queue, fallback, failure and shutdown contracts, then remove the remaining runtime donor extraction through sink/configuration callbacks.
4. **Logging provider:** reconcile conversion gating, formatter versions/ABI, float semantics and PPC control-flow behavior before moving the provider. Do not choose one consumer's behavior implicitly.
5. **Vertex/light, packing and cache consolidation:** first add modern-specific composition proof. Start with small coordinate/cache primitives; defer material formulas, complete combiner keys and metadata lifetime to explicit adapter contracts.
6. **Optional cleanup/packages:** entropy byte-fill and the trivial clock wrapper; codec/StormLib projectors where reuse justifies them; bounded archive reads only after both consumer policies are specified. Window/GUI/resource lifecycle stays local.

This classification closes the remaining-family inventory, not the migrations themselves. Fresh compilation and linking do not establish cross-consumer semantic equivalence or Xenos runtime fidelity. Complete regeneration from empty owned outputs, source-control/bootstrap deliverability, declared JSON/async semantic coverage, and exact-artifact Xenia/hardware validation remain open. No new migration percentage is inferred from file or translation-unit counts.
